Document: retry-policy-v3
Status: authoritative policy for this workshop fixture only

The workshop client accepts Retry-After as a non-negative ASCII integer number of seconds.
Zero means retry immediately. Leading and trailing whitespace may be stripped.
Missing, negative, fractional, HTTP-date, or malformed values use exponential backoff: 2 ** attempt.
Cap every delay at 12 seconds, including exponential backoff and valid Retry-After values.
The public retry_delay(attempt, retry_after=None) signature must stay unchanged.
Use only the Python standard library. Do not edit the tests. Tests must not sleep or contact the network.

This is a deliberately simplified exercise policy, not a complete HTTP Retry-After implementation.
